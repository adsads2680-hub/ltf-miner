MIMA Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information MIMA processes, why we use it, the services we rely on, and the choices available to users.

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Information MIMA processes

When you use MIMA inside Telegram, we may process information supplied by Telegram and information generated by your use of the Mini App, including:

  • Telegram user ID, username, and first name.
  • Mining, tapping, level, task, referral, reward, and balance activity.
  • Withdrawal/exchange requests, wallet address submitted for a withdrawal, transaction status, and blockchain transaction hash (TX Hash).
  • Technical timestamps and security/anti-abuse records needed to operate the service.

MIMA does not ask users for passwords, wallet seed phrases, or private keys.

2. How we use information

  • To provide mining, tapping, levels, tasks, referrals, rewards, balances, and withdrawals.
  • To verify Telegram sessions and protect accounts from fraud, duplicate reward claims, bots, and abuse.
  • To process and keep records of exchange/withdrawal requests.
  • To maintain service performance, security, and reliability.

3. Public payout proofs

Completed payouts may appear in the public “Withdrawals” section of MIMA. The public record can include a masked user label, payout amount, GRAM amount, completion time, and TX Hash so users can independently verify the blockchain transaction. We do not intentionally publish a user’s Telegram ID or withdrawal wallet address in this public list.

4. Advertising and AdsGram

MIMA displays optional rewarded ads through AdsGram. Watching an ad is not required to use the app's core features. A reward is granted only after the ad is completed successfully.

5. Service providers

MIMA uses third-party services to operate the Mini App. These may include Telegram for the Mini App platform and authentication, Supabase for backend/database infrastructure, GitHub Pages for static app hosting, and AdsGram for optional advertising. Each provider processes data under its own terms and privacy practices.

Telegram Privacy Policy: https://telegram.org/privacy
Supabase Privacy: https://supabase.com/privacy

6. Data retention and security

We keep account and transaction records for as long as reasonably necessary to operate MIMA, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, maintain payout records, and meet applicable legal obligations. We use server-side access controls and limit direct client access to sensitive database operations. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection to certain processing of your personal data.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as MIMA changes or as legal and service-provider requirements evolve. The latest version will be published at this page.
